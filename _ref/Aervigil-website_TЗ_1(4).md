- Hardware (рендер і зображення авто з системою на даху).
•Modular plug-and-play design — compact, lightweight, easy rooftop installation on partner vehicles
•NDIR method — CO/CO2 concentration measurements
•Chemiluminescence — NO/NO2/NOx/NH3/O3 measurements
•UV-fluorescence — SO2/H2S/TRS measurements
•Optical counter — PM1/PM2.5/PM5/PM10 and TSP dust monitoring
•Electrochemical sensors — additional pollutant measurement
•Li-Ion accumulator — 24/7 uninterrupted operation when vehicle power is unavailable
•Thermoelectric coolers — high-precision temperature stabilisation
•EHD-based air amplifier — continuous high-volume air sampling
During operation, air is continuously sampled from outside, heated to evaporate moisture and stabilise temperature, then measured simultaneously across all modules. Targeting EN 15267-4 certification — data legally recognised for EU regulatory reporting and ESG audits

•Automated CSRD/ESRS E1/E2 compliance report generation — PDF and Excel export
•ESG building passport — for BREEAM, LEED, EU Taxonomy and green financing

- AI engine (зображення пов’язане зAi або мапа міста з зонами)
Over 100,000 measurements per vehicle per year — processed through algorithmic validation combined with AI-assisted analysis and forecasting in real time:

01 — Data Validation
Filters dirty readings from tunnels, parking and heavy traffic. Cross-checks with nearby certified stations. Labels data by validity level.
02 — City Analysis
Identifies pollution hotspots and recurring patterns. Correlates data with meteorological parameters (wind, humidity, temperature). Detects morning peaks, seasonality, day/night cycles. Heatmap updated every 5–15 minutes. Street-level simulation is a long-term development goal following first large-scale deployment.
03 — Forecasting
24–72h air quality forecasts per specific location. Predicts norm exceedances before they occur. District-level pollution exposure analysis. Detection and mapping of anomalous pollution events.
04 — Recommendations & Optimization
Data for optimal placement of stationary sensors. Traffic rerouting suggestions to reduce pollution. Urban planning inputs: schools, parks, offices. Auto-generated CSRD/ESRS compliance reports.

- B2G
Municipalities & City Administrations
**Challenge**
Cities must monitor air quality across their entire territory — but stationary stations cover only 5–10% of urban areas and cost €50,000–€150,000 each.
**Solution**
Real-time air quality map of every street via partner fleets. Automated EU Air Quality Directive compliance reporting. Data for traffic management and urban planning.


- B2B
Industrial Enterprises & Logistics
**Challenge**
Under EU CSRD (mandatory from 2025), 50,000 companies must submit verified air quality data — most have no way to collect it.
**Solution**
Continuous monitoring around facilities and along fleet routes. Auto-generated CSRD/ESRS E1/E2 compliant reports ready for regulators and auditors.
Real Estate Developers
**Challenge**
ESG certification and green financing require verified environmental data for specific locations — often unavailable or expensive to obtain.
**Solution**
ESG building passport — a verified air quality profile for any location, supporting BREEAM, LEED and EU Taxonomy certification
- B2C
